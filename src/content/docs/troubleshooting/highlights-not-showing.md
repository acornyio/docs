---
title: Highlights are not showing up
description: Fix missing highlights after saving, importing, or syncing.
---

Use this checklist when you expected to see highlights in Acorny but they are missing.

## After saving with the extension

Confirm that you were signed in when you saved the highlight. Reload the Highlights page and check whether the source appears.

## After importing a file

Check the import result. If Acorny skipped rows, the file may contain empty highlights, unsupported records, or duplicates already in your library.

## After syncing a provider

Run a manual sync from Extensions & Apps. If the provider returns zero new highlights, confirm that the provider account has highlights or annotations available to sync.

## In Review

Highlights can appear in your library before they appear in Review. Review scheduling decides when a highlight becomes a recall card.
