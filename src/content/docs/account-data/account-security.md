---
title: Account security
description: Sign in, verify email, and reset your Acorny password.
---

Use account security pages when you cannot sign in or when Acorny asks you to verify your email address.

## Email verification

After creating an account, open the verification email and follow the link. Verification helps keep your account reachable for security and password recovery.

## Reset your password

Use Reset password on the sign-in page. Acorny sends a password reset email to your account address.

## Connected services

If you no longer want Acorny to sync from a connected provider, open Extensions & Apps and disconnect that provider.
