Acorny is currently in public beta. Pricing may change as the product matures, but saved highlights, notes, and review history remain central to the product experience.

## Current status

During public beta, Acorny is focused on capture, import, sync, and review workflows. If a pricing page or plan chooser is shown in the product, follow the current in-product information because it is the source of truth for account-specific availability.

This Help Center page explains the expected user-facing principles, not a billing contract.

## Saved highlights

Your saved highlights are the core data in Acorny. If pricing changes in the future, the product should continue to treat saved highlights as user data that remains accessible under the terms shown at that time.

If you are evaluating Acorny before a large migration, start with a small import and confirm the review workflow works for you before importing your full library.

## Beta expectations

Public beta means:

- Some provider integrations may still require setup or may change.
- Some self-serve account workflows may not be fully available yet.
- Documentation may be updated as product screens change.
- Pricing details may be clarified before general availability.

## Questions about pricing

If you need account-specific pricing help, contact support and include the email address for the account. Do not include passwords, access tokens, or payment details in a support message.
