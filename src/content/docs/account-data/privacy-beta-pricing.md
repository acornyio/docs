Acorny is built around your reading highlights, notes, and review history.

## Privacy

Acorny stores the highlights and account data needed to provide highlighting, import, sync, and review features. For the full policy, open the Privacy page on the main Acorny site.

## Data export

Self-serve data export is not a public Help Center workflow yet. If you need a copy of your data before a self-serve export is available, contact support from the Contact page.

## Beta pricing

Acorny is currently in public beta. If pricing changes in the future, saved highlights should remain accessible and reviewable as described on the Pricing page.
