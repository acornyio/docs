---
title: Sync from Inoreader
description: Connect Inoreader and sync annotated items into Acorny.
---

Use Inoreader sync when you save annotations in Inoreader and want those annotations to appear in Acorny.

## Connect Inoreader

Open Extensions & Apps in Acorny, find Inoreader, and choose Connect. Acorny sends you to Inoreader to authorize access, then returns you to Acorny.

## What sync imports

Acorny syncs annotated Inoreader items. It does not import every unread, starred, or account-level Inoreader item.

## If your annotations do not appear

Confirm that the source item is annotated in Inoreader, then run a manual sync from the integration card.
