---
title: Sync from Instapaper
description: Connect Instapaper and sync article highlights into Acorny.
---

Use Instapaper sync when you keep highlights in Instapaper and want Acorny to pull them into your library.

## Connect Instapaper

Open Extensions & Apps in Acorny, find Instapaper, and connect your account. Follow the prompts from Acorny and Instapaper to authorize the connection.

## What sync imports

Acorny syncs Instapaper highlights into your highlight library. It does not sync every Instapaper account setting or reading-progress field.

## If sync fails

Check that the Instapaper card still shows as connected, then run a manual sync. If the problem continues, disconnect and reconnect Instapaper.
